# Stillmere Hotel

A place to settle in.

Single-property hotel website concept: rooms, dining, our hotel, contact and direct booking. Guests select check-in, check-out and guest count, then choose a Classic Room, Deluxe Room or Suite within the same hotel.

## Visual direction

Warm, considered, welcoming. Navy #16324F, ivory #F6F3ED, blue #2563EB, brass #B39A72 and slate #475569. Cormorant Garamond for expressive headings; Inter for body copy and booking controls. Natural daylight, linen, oak and restrained brass details.

The hotel name is a proposed creative identity. Images, prices and facilities are illustrative concept content. The moodboard is generated artwork; typeface rendering is illustrative rather than a supplied font implementation.
