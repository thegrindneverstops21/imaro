# Hotel app moodboard — A stay you can trust

Direction: calm, clear and connected. Based on Naledi Mokoena's existing persona: nearby accommodation, budget clarity, accurate photography and straightforward mobile booking.

## Palette

| Colour | Hex | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deep Navy | #16324F | Headings and navigation |
| Trust Blue | #2563EB | Primary actions and links |
| Pale Blue | #EFF6FF | Backgrounds and highlights |
| White | #FFFFFF | Cards and button text |
| Slate Grey | #475569 | Supporting text |

## Visual direction

- Typography: Inter; clear sans-serif hierarchy and prominent total prices.
- Imagery: naturally lit, attainable urban hotels, practical workspaces and welcoming communal spaces. Generated images are conceptual references, not actual property photographs.
- Materials: white linen, warm wood and navy textiles.
- Components: softly rounded cards, familiar outline icons and a clear blue primary action.
- Mobile direction: stack content into one column; keep full price and booking action prominent, with visible keyboard focus and generously sized controls during implementation.
- Example room names and prices are illustrative.

## References

- [Laws of UX — proximity](https://lawsofux.com/): group price and fee information together.
- [Interfaces cheat sheet](https://interfaces.dev/cheat-sheet): consistent icon weight, optical alignment and stable price numerals.
- [Detail](https://detail.design/): general craft reference consulted.

## Generation

Created using the built-in image generation tool. The moodboard is a design concept, not an implemented or interaction-tested app.
