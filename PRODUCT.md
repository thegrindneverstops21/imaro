# Imaro

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

Help guests explore Imaro locations, compare rooms and prices, and book and manage a stay. Staff manage reservations, branches, rooms, and prices.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Existing React, TypeScript, Redux Toolkit and Vite frontend; Express API and PostgreSQL backend.
- Guest checkout remains available. Google sign-in supports account features.
- Prices are in South African rand. Availability, totals, policies and reservation status come from existing API contracts.
- Frontend is the current priority; backend polish is deferred. Preserve existing integrations and distinguish verified runtime behavior from implemented code.
- Confirmed 5 November 2026: work guest-first (homepage, rooms, booking), directly in React, in reviewable sprints.

## Brand Commitments

- Imaro is the current name. Stillmere artifacts are historical references.
- Preserve Cinzel display typography, Source Sans 3 interface/body typography and Hugeicons.
- Existing brief calls for a calm, photographic hotel experience with clear booking information.

## Evidence on Hand

Current preferences and agreed page scope are in docs/preferences.md. Existing briefs and concept images are in docs/design/ and assets/design/. Concept images are not proof of actual hotel facilities. Branch and room data include imagery and policies; their authenticity must not be overstated. Do not invent property claims, reviews, contact information or policies.

## Open Decisions

Final property photography, confirmed dining/contact content, and the detailed homepage composition remain to be resolved during the frontend sprints.
